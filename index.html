Pune 2050

Designing a Liveable Future

Transforming Pune into a sustainable and inclusive city by addressing key environmental, transport and social challenges.

Pune sits on the Mula–Mutha rivers, surrounded by hills, and is one of India's fastest‑growing education and tech hubs — today home to over 7 million people.

>

🌿 What this project shows

  • The main challenges facing Pune today: air pollution, congestion, flooding, water stress and social inequality.
  • A 2050 vision with cleaner mobility, cooler neighbourhoods and safer public space.
  • How these ideas link to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
Today's Problems

Major Urban Challenges

Pune's growth around the Mula–Mutha river system, expanding IT corridors and rising car ownership create pressure on air, water and streets.

Tap a card to reveal more

🌫️

Air Pollution

High PM levels from traffic and construction.

Pune's growing vehicle fleet and construction sites add fine dust and exhaust to the air, especially in busy corridors like Swargate and Hinjawadi.
🚗

Traffic Congestion

Slow, unreliable daily trips.

Many commuters travel from the outskirts to IT parks and universities, overloading radial roads and junctions with long peak‑hour delays.
🌧️

Flooding

Monsoon waterlogging in low‑lying areas.

Intense monsoon showers and encroached drains cause waterlogging near riverbanks and underpasses, blocking buses, two‑wheelers and emergency services.
🚰

Water Supply

Unequal access and stress on rivers.

While some gated communities receive regular supply, informal settlements face shortages, and pollution threatens river and groundwater quality.
🔥

Urban Heat

Concrete growth and little shade.

Loss of tree cover makes dense parts of Pune several degrees hotter than nearby rural areas, especially along main roads.
🏚️

Social Segregation

Separated housing and services.

High‑rise enclaves, informal settlements and older cores often have very different access to parks, schools, health care and safe public space.
The Vision

Solutions for Pune 2050

The 2050 vision keeps Pune walkable and liveable — cleaner mobility, cooler streets and mixed, inclusive neighbourhoods.

Tap a card to reveal more

🌳

Green Corridors

Tree‑lined streets and riverfront parks.

Continuous shade trees along main roads and a restored Mula–Mutha riverfront act as "cool corridors", lowering temperatures and improving air quality.
🚇

Metro Expansion

Fast routes between hubs.

A denser metro network links major campuses, IT parks and old city areas, so most daily commutes can happen by high‑capacity public transport.
💧

Water Management

Rain‑smart streets and storage.

Green drains, permeable pavements and neighbourhood‑level rainwater harvesting reduce flooding and recharge groundwater during the monsoon.
🏘️

Inclusive Neighborhoods

Mixed‑use, mixed‑income areas.

Housing, shops and services woven together, with safe public spaces and universal design so all age and income groups share the same city.
☀️

Renewable Energy

Solar Pune for 2050.

Rooftop solar on apartments, campuses and public buildings powers lighting, cooling and transport charging, cutting coal emissions.
🚴‍♀️

Active Mobility

Safe cycling and walking networks.

Protected cycle tracks and continuous footpaths connect metro stations, BRT stops and schools so short trips happen by bike or on foot.

Key Stats & Trends

How a shift towards public transport, better drainage and green infrastructure could change daily life in Pune by 2050.

By 2050, a much larger share of daily trips is made by metro, easing road congestion.

Cleaner vehicles and more trees push air quality towards healthier levels for residents.

Smart drainage and protected flood plains reduce monsoon street flooding.

Global Framework

UN Sustainable Development Goals

Pune 2050 is guided by several SDGs that link local actions to global climate and equality targets.

♀️

SDG 5 – Gender Equality

Well‑lit public spaces and safe transit routes for all genders at all times.

💧

SDG 6 – Clean Water

Cleaner rivers, better sewerage and neighbourhood rainwater harvesting.

⚖️

SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities

Mixed‑income housing and basic services within reach of every resident.

🏙️

SDG 11 – Sustainable Cities

Compact, transit‑oriented growth instead of endless low‑density sprawl.

🌍

SDG 13 – Climate Action

Lower emissions through public transport, green corridors and efficient buildings.

🏗️

SDG 9 – Innovation & Infrastructure

Modern, resilient infrastructure supporting Pune's tech and education role.
